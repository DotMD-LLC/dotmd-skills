# Version history and recovery

DotMD keeps document history so you can inspect earlier states and recover from unwanted changes. Retention can depend on the account plan and content type.

## Review history

1. Open the target item.
2. Open Version history.
3. Compare timestamps, authors, and available previews.
4. Identify the smallest version that contains the content you need.

## Restore safely

Restoring can replace the current state. Before restoring:

- confirm the exact item and version;
- read the current content first;
- preserve recent work if it is still needed;
- tell active collaborators;
- prefer copying a small recovered passage when a full restore is unnecessary.

After restoring, verify the intended content and any downstream links, formulas, comments, or published views.

## AI recovery rule

An AI may inspect and compare versions when authorized, but it must not restore one without explicit approval naming the target version. It should summarize what would be lost and what would return.
