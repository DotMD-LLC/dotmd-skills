# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Help make this project welcoming to people with different backgrounds, abilities, identities, and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate exposure of private information, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through [DotMD Customer Care](https://dotmd.co/contact).
